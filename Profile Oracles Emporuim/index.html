About

I believe every child (and the curious adult still inside them) deserves to experience wonder.

In a world that often rushes past the ordinary, I exist to reawaken curiosity — to help people notice the magic hidden in everyday things and to keep the sense of wonder alive.

I do this by blending storytelling, gentle mysticism, and interactive digital experiences. I create stories and apps that feel like small doorways into curiosity — combining education, imagination, and play in a warm, non-pushy way.

Under the Oracles Emporium brand, I create children’s books, audio stories, interactive apps, and short videos. These include collections like The Cabinet of Curiosities and mystical digital experiences designed to spark curiosity and learning through wonder.
